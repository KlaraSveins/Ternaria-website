Who we are

Engineering quality into mRNA performance

Ternaria designs the quality layer for therapeutic mRNA. How an mRNA is designed and produced shapes how it performs in the body, from potency to safety to reproducibility.

Why it matters

RNA performance is shaped long before it reaches the cell. RNA polymerase purity, IVT performance and construct design work together to determine RNA quality, translation, potency and safety.

What we do

We bring RNA design, enzyme innovation, and production together. We develop and manufacture ultra-pure RNA polymerases and purpose-built mRNA, powered by our proprietary platform that enables the next generation of RNA technologies.

Who we serve

We work with teams pushing RNA further, from academic research and therapeutic development to pharma R&D, manufacturing partners and CDMOs, across vaccines, oncology and rare disease.
